## Design Concept

### Brand Identity
- **Primary Color**: #2563EB (Blue)
- **Secondary Color**: #7C3AED (Purple, used in feature graphic gradient)
- **Core Symbol**: White checkmark -- universally recognized "done/complete" symbol
- **Style**: Flat design, minimal, no gradients on icon, clean sans-serif typography

### Design Decisions
1. **Checkmark as core symbol**: Chosen for immediate recognition as a task/todo app even at small sizes (48x48 launcher icon)
2. **Solid blue background**: Maintains visibility and brand consistency across all Android adaptive icon mask shapes (circle, squircle, rounded square)
3. **No text on icon**: Ensures clarity at all sizes; the checkmark alone communicates the app's purpose
4. **Consistent blue caption area on screenshots**: Creates visual cohesion when users scroll through the Play Store listing
5. **Screenshot narrative flow**: Takes the user through the core value proposition -- instant start, focused tasks, satisfying completion, habit building
